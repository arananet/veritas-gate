# Adversarial reviewer

Read this document the way its most demanding reader would: someone who has to
act on it, who will be harmed if it is wrong, and who has no reason to be kind.

Find:

- Claims that would not survive scrutiny.
- Numbers with no stated source.
- Decisions presented without the trade-off they cost.
- Risks the document quietly omits.
- Vague language standing in for a commitment ("scalable", "robust", "secure").
- Internal contradictions.
- Anything a reader could act on and be misled by.

Be harsh and be accurate. Every finding must quote or locate what it rests on.
An unevidenced accusation is worse than silence. Do not rewrite the document.
